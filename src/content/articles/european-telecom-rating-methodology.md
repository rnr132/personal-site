*Co-authored by Rohit Nair, Director, Corporate Ratings*

Scope Ratings has published its final methodology for rating European telecommunication services companies. It sits alongside Scope's General Corporate Rating Methodology and adds a more detailed, telecom-specific assessment of credit risk, with the aim of better credit differentiation across the sector. No comments were received from market participants during the call for comments, and the changes are not expected to have any rating impact.

The core of the methodology is how it assesses a telecom operator's competitive position, across three areas:

- **Market position**: scale of operations, market shares across geographies and segments, and how competitive those markets are.
- **Diversification**: exposure to different regions, customer types and segments.
- **Operating profitability**: Scope-adjusted EBITDA margin after leases, how volatile those margins are, and Scope-adjusted return on capital employed as a measure of capital efficiency that allows comparison across the industry.

It also assesses whether the regulatory framework an operator works under helps or hurts its business risk profile. A stable, well-developed framework would typically be neutral, while regulation that is, or is reasonably likely to start, weighing on operations and cash flows could lead to a negative adjustment.
