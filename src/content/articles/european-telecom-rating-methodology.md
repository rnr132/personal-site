*By Rohit Nair, Director, Corporate Ratings*

A colleague and I have just published Scope Ratings' final European Telecommunication Services Rating Methodology. Until now, we rated telecom operators under our General Corporate Rating Methodology, which covers every industry. The new methodology sits on top of that and adds a telecom-specific view of credit risk, so we can tell operators apart more precisely. It isn't expected to change any existing ratings.

Most of it is about one question: how strong is an operator's competitive position? We look at three things.

- **Market position.** How big the operator is, its market share in each country and segment, and how hard-fought those markets are.
- **Diversification.** How spread it is across regions, customer types and segments. An operator in one country is exposed in a way a multi-market group isn't.
- **Operating profitability.** EBITDA margin after leases shows how efficiently it runs, and how much that margin swings over time matters too. Return on capital employed shows how well it uses its capital, and lets us compare operators across the industry.

We also look at regulation, which matters a lot in telecoms. A stable, well-developed framework is usually neutral. But if regulation is hurting an operator's operations and cash flows, or is reasonably likely to, that can count against its business risk profile.
